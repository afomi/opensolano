Parks Spending

What it costs to run Vacaville's parks, per park and per acre.

These are deliberately simple, objective metrics — not a perfect cost model. The goal is to give residents and commissioners intuitive numbers they can reason about: roughly what does a park cost, and how does spending compare across parks of different sizes.

Parks money lives in three places in the budget, so looking at only the "Parks & Recreation Department" line undercounts the real cost by nearly half. The totals below combine all three.

How to read these numbers

  • Cost per park hides size. A pocket park and a regional park both count as one. Cost per acre is the fairer peer comparison.
  • Not every assessment zone is a park. Some are business-park or drainage districts, marked "non-park" above and excluded from the park metrics.
  • Per-zone direct cost is not fully loaded. Shared staff salaries and indirect costs aren't pushed down to individual parks, so these figures understate true per-park cost.
  • Acreage is computed from the city's GIS polygon geometry, not a published park inventory, so a few parks may differ slightly from signage.

Sources

  • Budget figures: City of Vacaville FY2025-26 Final Budget Document — Parks & Recreation Department, Parks Maintenance Division (Public Works), and Lighting & Landscaping Districts (printed p.123).
  • Acreage: City of Vacaville GIS parks shapefile, areas computed from polygon geometry (State Plane CA Zone II, US survey feet).
