About this project

These signs were posted by a Vacaville resident as a non-commercial community project. The City of Vacaville did not create, fund, or endorse them.

Feedback is read by one person and, when useful, raised with City staff or in public comment at a public meeting. It is never sent to Parks and Recreation Commissioners as a group.
