# Personas

## resident

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Name** | Resident |
| **Role** | Solano County resident and taxpayer |
| **Responsibilities** | Votes on local measures, attends (or follows) city council meetings, pays taxes and fees |
| **Goals** | Understand how local government spends public money; stay informed about decisions that affect their neighborhood; compare their city to neighboring jurisdictions |
| **Context** | Browses on mobile during evenings or weekends. May arrive via social media link or local news reference. Not a data professional — needs plain-language summaries with optional drill-down into raw numbers |

## researcher

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Name** | Researcher |
| **Role** | Journalist, academic, or policy analyst covering Solano County |
| **Responsibilities** | Produces reports, articles, or analyses using municipal data |
| **Goals** | Access raw, downloadable datasets; cross-reference data across jurisdictions and time periods; cite authoritative sources |
| **Context** | Works on desktop with spreadsheets or data tools. Wants CSV/JSON downloads, clear data provenance, and "last updated" dates. Arrives with a specific question and expects to find (or request) the data to answer it |

## volunteer

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Name** | Volunteer |
| **Role** | Civic-minded community member willing to contribute time |
| **Responsibilities** | Attends public meetings, takes notes, submits data, or helps with outreach |
| **Goals** | Find a concrete way to contribute that matches their skills and availability; see the impact of their work on the site |
| **Context** | Discovers OpenSolano through the meetup or word of mouth. Needs a clear on-ramp: what to do, how to do it, and what "done" looks like. May have no technical background |

## meetup_attendee

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Name** | Meetup Attendee |
| **Role** | Local creator, technologist, or curious resident attending the Solano Creative Tech Meetup |
| **Responsibilities** | Shows up, participates in discussion, optionally collaborates on projects |
| **Goals** | Connect with like-minded people in Solano County; learn about civic tech and peer-to-peer tools; find project ideas |
| **Context** | Arrives at the meetup page to find the next date, location, and what to expect. May sign up via the Airtable form. Engagement is in-person first, digital second |

## site_maintainer

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Name** | Site Maintainer |
| **Role** | OpenSolano operator responsible for content, data updates, and technical health |
| **Responsibilities** | Publishes blog posts, updates datasets, manages collections, monitors SEO and site performance |
| **Goals** | Keep the site fresh and trustworthy; grow audience through search and social; make data updates easy and error-free |
| **Context** | Works in the Jekyll codebase and GitHub. Needs efficient workflows for content publishing, data ingestion, and deployment. Cares about structured data, RSS, and analytics to measure impact |
