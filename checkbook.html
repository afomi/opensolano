Public Budget Transparency

Follow the checks, not the press release.

This explorer reads the April 8, 2026 checkbook export in the browser and turns it into something closer to an operating room than a spreadsheet. Track total dollars by vendor, total dollars by fund, and how transaction volume tells a different story than raw spend.

Source file
checkbook_data_2026-04-08.csv

Load the raw file, inspect the biggest relationships, and click any row below to spotlight that vendor or fund.

Exported 2026-04-08 from the City of Vacaville's open-spending portal (cityofvacaville-ca.spending.socrata.com), a Socrata-hosted checkbook of vendor payments by fund. Columns: Vendor, Fund, Check #, Date, Amount.
