A Vacaville Neighborhood Association (VNA) is a resident-led group with defined boundaries, a chair and co-chair, and a registration with the City. Membership is free, voluntary, and open to residents, renters, property owners, and businesses inside the boundaries — it is not an HOA. The program launched in 2023 under the City's COV Connect engagement effort.

To reach an association's leadership, contact the City's VNA team at VNA@cityofvacaville.com or (707) 449-5100 — they connect residents with the chair of the association covering their address.

Start one: five steps

  1. Draw your boundaries. Name the streets that form the northern, southern, eastern, and western edges of your neighborhood, and sketch a simple map.
  2. Pick a name and a purpose statement. The name must be unique and appropriate. The purpose statement should be brief and broad — for example, "To provide a safe, clean neighborhood we're proud to live in."
  3. Choose a chair and co-chair. Both must live within the boundaries. The City provides standard bylaws you can adapt.
  4. Register with the City. Submit the registration form with your contacts, name, purpose, boundaries, and map. Staff follows up, and your association is added to the official map.
  5. Meet at least once a year. That's the only ongoing requirement. Membership must stay open to everyone within the boundaries.

What registration unlocks

  • A direct line to City Hall — a recognized channel for raising neighborhood concerns collectively.
  • Mini-grants up to $1,000 for community engagement and neighborhood improvement projects.
  • The equipment loan program — free borrowed tools and supplies for cleanup and beautification events, with liability waivers already worked out. (application)
  • The VNA toolkit — the City's organizing guide, in English and Spanish.

The City's own suggested activities for associations include block parties, cleanup projects, community picnics — and adopting a park. Several registered associations are already organized around a park or green space.
