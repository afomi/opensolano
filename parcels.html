Parcels

Exploring feedback, information, and action

Every city has parcels sitting idle — vacant lots, empty storefronts, underused spaces. Information about them exists in public records, but it's not connected to the people who can do something about it.

This project surfaces that latent information, collects public feedback, and connects places to people whose interests can bring a parcel to life.

Pick a parcel. Tell us what you'd like to see there.

Not endorsed by the City of Vacaville or property owners. Built from public assessor records as a civic interest project.
